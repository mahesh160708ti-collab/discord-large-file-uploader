Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with our Discord bot and web uploader.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord User ID & Server ID: Used solely to associate uploaded files with their uploader, track active storage quotas (5 GB ceiling), and handle permissions.
  • File Metadata: Original filename, size in bytes, MIME content type, and upload timestamp.
  • Hashed IP Addresses: We do NOT store raw IP addresses. Downloader and abuse reporter IP addresses are cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

2. Data Retention & Automatic Purge

All stored files, download links, and associated metadata are strictly temporary. When a file expires or hits its download cap, it is automatically and permanently purged from object storage by our background lifecycle worker.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers. Files are stored securely in zero-egress object storage (Cloudflare R2) and are only accessible by individuals possessing the specific random public download link.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For inquiries, support, or privacy concerns, please contact the bot administrators via the official Discord support server or project repository.
